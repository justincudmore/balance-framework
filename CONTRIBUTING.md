# Contributing to The Balance Framework

The Balance Framework is an open, living architecture. Contributions, challenges, and extensions are welcome.

---

## Ways to Contribute

**Challenge the architecture**
If you believe a drive is misplaced, missing, or redundant — open an issue. The framework should survive intellectual pressure. If it doesn't, it needs to evolve.

**Extend the interaction rules**
The interaction effects in `engine/balance.py` are a starting set. New interaction rules that produce demonstrably distinct emergent archetypes are welcome as pull requests.

**Build on the data structure**
`drives.json` is the single source of truth. Build tools, visualizations, or implementations on top of it. Attribution required per the CC BY 4.0 license.

**Translate the framework**
If you can translate the framework definitions into another language accurately — not just literally — that extends the reach of this work meaningfully.

**Connect it to empirical research**
The framework was built from first principles. Connecting its drives and tier structure to peer-reviewed personality psychology, cognitive science, or AI research strengthens it. Cite your sources.

---

## What We Are Not Looking For

- Contributions that flatten the generative architecture into a descriptive one
- Additions that duplicate existing drives under different names
- Changes to Tier 1 — the conditions for consciousness are locked by design

---

## How to Submit

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a branch with a descriptive name
3. Make your changes with clear commit messages
4. Open a pull request with a summary of what changed and why

---

## Attribution

All contributions must be compatible with CC BY 4.0. By contributing you agree your additions may be shared under the same license, with appropriate credit to all contributors.
